# Trio quest branching smell test — 2026-10-17

**Westfall implementation update:** the first visit now owns pickups 9, 12, 22, 36, 38, 64, 102, 151 and 153, plus deliveries 36 and 109. The later duplicate block and its farm-travel helper were removed; the first visit's duplicate pickup of 12 was consolidated, and the Gryan/Danuvin steps no longer depend on `#era`. Later completion objectives, other turn-ins and oats tracking were preserved. Static checks confirmed single ownership for all four profiles and valid local label references. No in-game validation was available. The findings, counts and ledger below describe the original research snapshot, before this implementation and other subsequent edits.

The scan finds substantial repeated authoring, but **no confirmed case of the same quest pickup or turn-in being assigned to two different NPCs**. Most repetitions are the same NPC on different visits, class-specific reward choices, party shares, or item-start recovery. Two concrete problems emerged: an unbounded optional item-start pickup, and reward branches missing their condition delimiters. Repeated chapter ownership is a separate cleanup opportunity, rather than proof that players perform a quest twice.

## Scope and method

- Reviewed the current working files, including the untracked `Custom-Forever-24-26-Deadmines.lua` and recent chapter reorder. Existing edits and older audit artifacts were preserved.
- The Forever manifest loads 22 custom files: 21 files containing 22 literal registered guides, plus the dynamically generated City Supplies template. This includes the supplementary 34–35 Dalaran Stormwind delivery guide, although it is selected manually rather than reached through the main chapter chain.
- Included the shared `32-33 Hillsbrad/Arathi@fly2` tail. City Supplies includes contain no quest accepts or turn-ins. Did not count the entire default leveling route after the trio handoff.
- Scanned explicit `.accept` and `.turnin` commands by **quest ID + action**, normalizing negative turn-in IDs. A pickup and a turn-in of one quest are expected and are not a duplicate pair. Different quest IDs sharing a title are not merged.
- Evaluated class/race selectors for Dwarf Paladin, Dwarf Warrior, Gnome Warrior and Gnome Warlock. Warrior race choices are alternatives. Numeric level selectors are included as an all-levels envelope. Quest state, optionality, inventory, season and movement are retained for review rather than simulated.
- Inspected every duplicate group and compared NPCs, coordinates, item starts and surrounding gates. Different map-coordinate spellings were not treated as different locations automatically; e.g. `StormwindClassic` aliases the city map, and the Milton and Rugelfuss world/percentage variants refer to the same stops.

## Findings

### 1. Grant's Shield has an optional pickup that can stop the route

At [Duskwood Return:176](../../Guides/Forever/Custom-Forever-22-Duskwood-Return.lua#L176), the Raven Hill pickup for **79362, Grant's Shield**, has `.isQuestAvailable 79362`, `.goto Duskwood,22,43`, `.use 281147` and `.accept 79362`. Its prose says to continue if Lost Knight is absent. There is no `#completewith`, `#optional`, item-count gate or completing proximity radius on that step.

Confirmed engine behavior: `.isQuestAvailable` checks turn-in history, not whether the NPC/item is available; `.accept` completes once the quest is accepted or turned in. Consequently, a player who has not received the shield can reach this step with an unfinished accept objective despite the instruction to continue. This is a static route-flow defect; a player can still skip the step manually.

The other two pickup opportunities are bounded by `#completewith next`: [earlier in Duskwood Return:124](../../Guides/Forever/Custom-Forever-22-Duskwood-Return.lua#L124) and [Duskwood Third Pass:101](../../Guides/Forever/Custom-Forever-24-25-Duskwood-Third-Pass.lua#L101). Multiple item-start opportunities are reasonable; this particular opportunity needs a matching escape condition. Choose an item-in-bags gate with a bounded rare check, or a deliberate `#completewith` endpoint, according to the intended stop.

### 2. Red Leather Bandanas has two overlapping reward branches

**Implemented:** added `<< Warrior` and `<< !Warrior` to the two reward lines and increased the Westfall guide version to 27. Static selector checks confirm one turn-in per trio profile: reward 2 for Warriors and reward 3 for Paladin/Warlock. In-game reward selection was not tested. The description below records the original finding.

[Westfall & Redridge:703–704](../../Guides/Forever/Custom-Forever-16-18-Westfall-Redridge.lua#L703) contains:

```text
.turnin 153,2 >> Turn in Red Leather Bandanas Warrior
.turnin 153,3 >> Turn in Red Leather Bandanas !Warrior
```

Both lines apply to every character. `Warrior` and `!Warrior` are ordinary display text without `<<`. This is a confirmed missing-condition defect, at one location rather than two. It creates competing reward instructions; which reward the live automation chooses was not tested.

The intended form appears to be `<< Warrior` and `<< !Warrior`, as used by the shipped-style reward alternatives elsewhere in the trio. Do not infer two actual turn-ins: both objectives refer to the same quest completion state.

## Repetitions that should not automatically fail the smell test

- **Class splits and reward choices:** 20 quest/action groups are mutually exclusive for the profiles scanned. Examples include Fargodeep Mine 62, Report to Gryan 109, Hogger 176, Culling the Threat 1054, Frosthowl 98326, and class-specific reward indices. Each character sees one alternative.
- **Item-start quests:** 373, 79362 and 95213 can be started from a carried item in more than one place. Dungeon/field pickup plus later bag recovery is not conflicting NPC geography. Grant's Shield still has the unbounded-step problem above.
- **Late drops/materials:** the powder 95213/95214 recovery, Old History Book 337 / Southshore 538, Bronze Tube chain, and late BFD manuscript 971 delivery support differing inventory/progress states.
- **Sticky opportunity followed by a fixed stop:** 414/1339 at patrolling Kadrell and 98322/98319 at Gretchen use an early `#completewith` opportunity followed by a definite interaction. These are intentional flow patterns, not two required transactions.
- **Earlier optional completion:** early and final Saldean turn-ins, Trogg Threat 267, Darkshore 986/993/995, and timed mug recovery 3364/3365 reuse the same giver/recipient. An already-completed quest skips its repeated objective.
- **Same NPC, alternative coordinate syntax:** textual coordinate inequality is too noisy to establish a second location. Target helpers can also be attached with `+` in a step containing several NPCs; the first or last waypoint alone is insufficient.

## Parser evidence and validation limits

Confirmed from installed code:

- `GuideLoader.lua:36`: selector syntax uses slash OR, whitespace AND, negation and parentheses.
- `GuideLoader.lua:874` and `:900`: `<<` supplies a selector; `>>` supplies display text. A class name inside display text does not filter a command.
- `functions.lua:1153`: accept objectives complete when accepted or already turned in.
- `functions.lua:1369`: negative turn-ins skip absent quests; completed quest history also completes repeated normal turn-in objectives.
- `functions.lua:4320`, `:4367`, `:4418`, `:4480`: quest-state gates skip entire steps when their tests fail; `.isQuestAvailable` is the reverse of turned-in history.
- `RXPGuides.lua:2572`: `#era` is excluded for the SoM setting; it is not a universal exclusion on Forever. The raw ledger retains these conditional lines.
- `map.lua:1534`: the installed map resolver handles `StormwindClassic`/city aliases.

Compared patterns with the manifest-loaded `Alliance-1-14_DwarfGnome.lua` (powder recovery and ordinary `<<` reward branches) and `Alliance-11-20.lua`, plus the shared Hillsbrad include source. The filtered default Duskwood/Redridge source also repeats the Stockades 387 pickup, supporting the interpretation that its gated-plus-fallback pair is intentional convention.

The scanner checks current source, not stale generated quest data. It expands the one non-city include used by this route and rejects unhandled includes or alternative quest-action primitives. It does not execute the addon parser, simulate movement, quest drops, server availability or all runtime settings. No in-game reload or character walkthrough was available. No route files were changed.
